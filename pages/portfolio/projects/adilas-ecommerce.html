Adilas E-commerce

A redesign and expansion of the Adilas E-commerce platform - over 100 new configuration settings and a modern, Bootstrap-based visual refresh - one of my first large ColdFusion/backend engagements for AFB.

Quick Facts
RoleDeveloper
ClientAdilas
TechColdFusion, MySQL, Bootstrap, HTML5/CSS, FooTable
Scope100+ new settings, full visual redesign

The Problem

Adilas E-commerce needed a modern look and feel and significantly more configurability than it had, including email functionality for invoices and quotes.

My Role

Developer on the redesign and settings expansion - one of my first major ColdFusion/backend projects for AFB.

What I Built

  • Over 100 new configuration settings for merchants.
  • A complete visual restyle of the storefront using the Bootstrap framework.
  • Email functionality for invoices and quotes sent directly from the platform.

Technologies & Approach

ColdFusion and MySQL on the backend, Bootstrap for the responsive redesign, and HTML5/CSS with tools like FooTable for data-heavy tables.

ColdFusion MySQL Bootstrap HTML5 / CSS3 FooTable

Challenges

This was one of my first large ColdFusion/backend projects, so the challenge was ramping up on an unfamiliar language and an existing, already-live e-commerce platform at the same time - while still shipping a big batch of new settings and a full redesign.

How They Were Solved

I leaned on Bootstrap's conventions to move fast on the visual redesign while learning ColdFusion and MySQL deeply enough to safely extend the existing data model with the new settings, testing incrementally against the live platform rather than attempting one large-batch change.

Outcome & Value Created

A modernized storefront with substantially more merchant configurability and built-in quote/invoice emailing - and a project that stretched my ColdFusion, MySQL, and Bootstrap skills considerably going into later, larger projects.
