Heaven is worth It!

Keep in mind, Heaven is more than you can wish, more than you can imagine, and more than you could ever conceive.

Preference

I wrote this book so that we “might have life, and that [we] might have it more abundantly” I wish to increase your hope and understanding. That you may know God and to know Him more abundantly.

And this is life eternal, that they might know thee the only true God, and Jesus Christ, whom thou hast sent.

That you may see His signature in all righteous things. And if the things I write contradict God or His teachings choose the authoritative path of Apostles and Prophets. Choose the word of God over my words.

I share the lessons I have learned from God and His words. These are the most important things I can share with the world.

“WE ALL IMAGINE GOD. Some imagine God as a myth or a fairy tale. Others imagine God as distant and scary, like a harsh judge or demanding parent. Still others imagine God as the most beautiful, loving, benevolent being in the universe. How you and I imagine God matters because it influences us, for better or worse, more than anything else we can think about. It shapes how we view ourselves, others, and our very purpose for existing.” - Imagine the God of Heaven by John Burke

John Burke in his book. Imagine the God of Heaven talks about the research and scientific study about near-death experiences (NDEs). “Here were people who clinically died-had no heartbeat or brain waves-yet modern medicine (or miracle) brought them back to life. When they revived, they claimed to have felt more alive than ever in an exhilarating world beyond our earthly space-time dimensions.”

The reason I care about these things is it paints a fuller picture to me about heaven. In the doctrines of the Church of Jesus Christ of latter-day saints, we have many quotes about heaven, but they are all generic and usually do not include any details about what heaven is like. If you took the quotes from our church, you can come up with the conclusion of this.

Heaven is more than I can wish, more than I can imagine, and more than I could ever conceive. Heaven may not be what I imagine, but it most certainly will be more than I can imagine.
